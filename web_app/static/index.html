01 Straighten the lens

Align a straight barrier with the guide grid. Start with radial correction.

Tilt: negative rotates left, positive rotates right. Rotation can leave black corners. Lens or tilt changes clear road points, the measurement line and car references; finish these adjustments first.

02 Draw the road

Click 4 or more points around the road boundary. Finish, or continue to detection to save it automatically. Drag dots to refine.

Top edge = far. Bottom edge = near. Draw around the surface, including wheel contact positions.

03 Choose where to measure

Place a vertical line where cars appear side-on. Only the center of a car's bbox triggers a measurement.

Play opens continuous video and GPU detection in the operator window. The measurement line filters measurements without pausing playback.

Metric rulers · variable scale

Mark real, equally spaced distances along the road in the corrected image. Click every measured metre (or enter another spacing) from left to right, including center and edges.

Existing marks can be dragged; edit each ruler’s spacing below. Use at least 3 marks per ruler. One ruler covers only its lane depth (±5% of road depth); add another along the near/far lane to cover the area between them. Cover the full vehicle length. Measurements outside coverage are withheld. Rulers take priority over car references. Finish lens correction first; lens or road changes invalidate calibration.

Marks must be physically measured on the road, parallel to vehicle travel, at wheel contact depth. Drawing equal screen distances does not calibrate the camera. Display resizing preserves original pixel coordinates. Validate with independently measured, side-on vehicles; body height, occlusion and camera angle still affect bbox accuracy.

04 Set a known car length

Detect and click a car, or draw its box. Set its measured length using the slider.

Reference lengths loaded from JSON can be edited below. Use at least two known cars at different depths. Camera height (6 m) and road offset (12 m) alone do not establish pixel scale.

Выберите автомобиль и отправьте измерение на проверку. Повторная отправка того же bbox в том же кадре не создаёт дубль.

Оператор

Продолжить калибровку или начать новую

JSON можно загрузить до изображения. При обновлении кадра разметка сохраняется. Используйте ту же камеру и разрешение. Для применения настройки IP-камер сначала остановите камеры в окне оператора; редактирование по снимку не останавливает измерение.
